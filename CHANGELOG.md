# Changelog

All notable changes to this project. Format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Project scaffold: `src/`, `tests/`, `configs/`, `notebooks/`, `data/`, `results/`, `artifacts/`, and `run_all.sh`.
- `pyproject.toml` with runtime dependencies, a `dev` extra, and `ruff` and `pytest` settings.
- `configs/config.yaml` (seed, test size, number of folds) and `src/utils.py` with `load_config` and `set_seed`.
- Frozen test split and 5-fold CV indices in `data/split.csv` (`make_split`, `save_split`, `load_split` in `src/lib.py`).
- `evaluate_regression` (RMSE, MAE, R²) with a test against `sklearn.metrics` to 1e-9.
- `build_pipeline` (scaler plus model, or a bare tree) with a test that the scaler is refit on each training fold.
- Determinism test: same seed gives an identical split and identical tiny-training predictions.
- `notebooks/setup_baselines.ipynb`: EDA on the training portion, `results/fig1_eda.png`, and the baselines table in `results/baselines.csv` (mean, ridge, depth-6 tree).
- `notebooks/error_correlation.ipynb`: voters simulation (21 independent voters reach 82.7%, 21 identical copies stay at 60.1%) and error correlation of the trees in one random forest (correlation 0.39, single-tree RMSE 0.806, forest RMSE 0.508), a `max_features` sweep with a `bootstrap=False` extreme case (`results/error_correlation_sweep.csv`), and `results/fig2_error_correlation.png`.
- Search budget in `configs/config.yaml` (`tune.n_trials: 20`, `tune.time_cap_seconds: 900`), the same for LightGBM and the MLP, set before any search is run.
- `notebooks/lightgbm_vs_mlp.ipynb`: LightGBM random search over seven settings, scored by the shared 5-fold CV with early stopping on an inner slice of each training fold. A 3-trial run logs one row per trial (parameters, CV mean and std, trial seconds, cumulative seconds).
- Same notebook: PyTorch MLP random search (width, depth, learning rate, weight decay, dropout) with standardized features and target, Adam, MSE and early stopping. One shared `run_search` loop writes the same log columns for both models, and RMSE is reported in $100k, directly comparable with LightGBM.
- `src/tune.py` (`python -m src.tune --model lgbm` or `--model mlp`): both searches as a CLI that stops at `n_trials` or the time cap, with `tests/test_tune.py`. Real 20-trial runs wrote `results/trials_{lgbm,mlp}.csv` and `results/best_{lgbm,mlp}.json` (best CV RMSE 0.4492 for LightGBM, 0.5184 for the MLP).
- `notebooks/lightgbm_vs_mlp.ipynb` now loads the search results: budget curves in `results/fig3_equal_budget.png` and a report in `results/tuning_summary.csv` (best settings refit on the full training portion, test set scored once: LightGBM RMSE 0.441, MLP 0.530, both under 20 trials). Search code moved out of the notebook into `src/tune.py`, with a `fit_predict_lgbm` helper.
- `notebooks/distillation.ipynb`: expected share of the teacher's gain kept by each student, written before running any distillation.
- `AverageEnsemble` and `EarlyStoppingLGBM` in `src/lib.py` (with tests, including a joblib round trip), and the teacher in `notebooks/distillation.ipynb`: the average of the tuned LightGBM and a 300-tree random forest. Test RMSE is 0.458, lower than the forest (0.494) but not LightGBM alone (0.441), because their errors are 0.92 correlated.
- `out_of_fold_predictions` in `src/lib.py` (with a test) and out-of-fold teacher labels for all 16,512 training rows in `data/teacher_oof.csv`. Teacher RMSE against the true labels is 0.464 out-of-fold but 0.239 in-sample.
- `make_synthetic_rows` in `src/lib.py` (with tests) and 82,560 synthetic rows (5x the training rows) labeled by the teacher in `notebooks/distillation.ipynb`, with a `distill` section in `configs/config.yaml`. Latitude and longitude get 2% jitter and the other features 5%. A nearest-real-row check shows the synthetic rows are no more spread out than the real ones.
- `make_students` in `src/lib.py` (with a test) and the three students in `notebooks/distillation.ipynb`: MLPs with 2 hidden layers of 32 and 128, and a depth-6 tree, each one object with `fit` and `predict`. Student settings are in `configs/config.yaml`.
- The three training arms (true labels, out-of-fold teacher labels, teacher labels plus synthetic rows) for all three students, run once on one seed in `notebooks/distillation.ipynb`. MLPs keep 40% and 59% of the teacher's gain in the synthetic-rows arm, the tree about 1%.
- `src/distill.py` (`python -m src.distill`): 3 students x 3 arms x 5 seeds = 45 fits, with `tests/test_distill.py`. Writes `results/distill_runs.csv` (test RMSE, fidelity, training seconds per fit), `results/distill_sq_errors.npy` (squared error of every test row per fit, same order as the CSV) and `results/distill_teacher.json`. Re-running a seed gives identical results.
- `paired_bootstrap_rmse` in `src/lib.py` (with a test) and `notebooks/distillation_results.ipynb`: mean ± std per student and arm, and the share of the teacher's gain kept with a paired-bootstrap 95% interval (`results/distill_summary.csv`). With teacher labels plus synthetic rows the 128-wide MLP keeps 62% (56% to 68%), the 32-wide MLP 35% (29% to 40%) and the tree 0.3%. Teacher labels alone add nothing measurable.
- Cost accounting in `notebooks/distillation_results.ipynb` (`results/cost_teacher.csv`, `cost_models.csv`, `cost_all_in.csv`), measured on an Apple M5 Pro: the teacher costs 619 s once (555 s of it tuning), the synthetic-rows students train in 6 to 15 s, and a student predicts 125 to 400 times faster per row than the teacher (batch).
- Size, speed and memory in `notebooks/size_speed_memory.ipynb` (`results/size_speed_memory.csv`), one row per model: the teacher and each student's best arm, saved by the new `src/build_artifacts.py` as `joblib` files in `artifacts/` (git-ignored). `src/bench.py` (parameter counts, thread pinning, single-row latency, a numpy-only MLP forward pass) and `src/measure_memory.py` (fresh-process memory), with tests. On an Apple M5 Pro the teacher is 418 MB and takes 5.5 ms per row; the 128-wide MLP is 0.42 MB and takes 124 microseconds (5 microseconds for the model alone).
- Shipping each model as one artifact: `python -m src.build_artifacts` now also writes a model card per model (`artifacts/<name>.model_card.json`: git commit, date, test metrics, each feature's min and max, file hash, library versions) and a serialization check (`check_rows.npy` and `<name>.check_pred.npy`). New tests reload every artifact in a separate process, predict 100 saved rows and require predictions identical to the in-memory model, and check each card against its file. All four artifacts pass.
- `README.md` rewritten as a project summary with the headline result, `results/fig4_distillation.png` (share of the teacher's gain kept, with intervals, built by `notebooks/report_figures.ipynb`), the cost table and the error-correlation figure. `run_all.sh` now runs the two searches, the 45 distillation fits, the artifact build and the tests.
